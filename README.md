## Amazon Global Superstore Sales Analytics (Power BI)

An interactive business intelligence dashboard to analyze Amazon’s sales, profit, quantity, and returns across multiple years, categories, cities, customers, and products.

---

##  Overview

This dashboard helps stakeholders quickly understand business performance by tracking key financial metrics and identifying top-performing cities, customers, and products. It converts raw sales data into meaningful insights for better decision-making.

---
##  Key Questions Answered

How much total sales and profit is generated?

Which categories and segments perform best?

Which cities contribute the most profit?

Who are the most profitable customers?

Which products and sub-categories generate maximum profit?

How does sales performance change year over year?


##  Dashboard Features

✔ Total Sales, Total Profit, Quantity, Returns KPIs
✔ Sales by Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
✔ Sales by Category (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies)
✔ Profit by City and Category
✔ Top Customers by Profit
✔ Top Products by Profit
✔ Profit by Sub-Category
✔ Year filter for time-based analysis
